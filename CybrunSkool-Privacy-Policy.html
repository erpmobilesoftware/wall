At a glance. Cybrun Skool is an offline-capable marks-entry app for teachers and other authorized staff at participating schools. Schools provision users and student records through their desktop school management system. The app needs an internet connection for initial school validation and data setup; thereafter, authorized users can enter marks offline and synchronize changes when connected. The participating school, not ERPMobile, decides how its school records are managed.

1. About this policy

This Privacy Policy describes how information is handled when an authorized user operates Cybrun Skool (the “App”), developed by ERPMobile (the “Developer”). It applies to the App and the school information that the App accesses, stores locally, or exchanges through its synchronization functions.

Cybrun Skool is intended for schools that have already been onboarded to the Cybrun school management system. It is not a public self-registration service, and it is not intended for use by children as account holders.

2. Responsibility for school data

The participating school is responsible for its school database and the personal information in it. Each school determines which learners, teachers, subjects, assessments, accounts, and records are created; who may access them; and how long they are retained, corrected, or deleted, subject to applicable law.

ERPMobile develops and maintains the App and owns the App software, design, branding, and associated assets. Ownership of the software does not mean ownership of a school's student, staff, marks, or other institutional records. ERPMobile does not have independent authority to decide how a school uses or deletes those records.

Where ERPMobile provides technical infrastructure or support that processes school information on the school's behalf, that processing should be governed by the school's instructions and applicable agreements. Technical involvement does not transfer control of the school's records to ERPMobile.

3. Information processed through the App

Depending on the school's configuration and the user's authorization, the App may process:

  • School setup information: system ID, school identification and configuration details, and the current academic period.
  • Staff and account information: school-created user accounts, identifiers, assigned roles or permissions, and information used to validate access and sign in.
  • Learner and academic information: student names and identifiers, classes or streams, subjects, examination sets or assessments, and related academic records provided by the school.
  • Marks and synchronization information: marks entered by authorized teachers, associated learner/subject/assessment references, and information needed to identify and synchronize changes.

The App is designed for school-authorized academic workflows. It does not offer public account sign-up, and teachers are not expected to register students in the mobile App: student records originate from the school's main management system.

Important: The App may handle personal information about learners, including minors, even though the intended App users are adults. A school's authorization to use the App does not remove its obligations to protect learner information.

4. Internet connectivity, local storage, and synchronization

First-time setup

Internet access is required during initial setup to validate the school's system ID and retrieve the relevant school data, including the current academic period, from the connected cloud database.

Accounts and login

Users are created and managed through the school's desktop management system. Account information is synchronized as part of the school's connected data workflow. Authorized users must log in to use protected functions in the App; the App does not provide independent account creation.

Offline marks entry

After the required setup and data synchronization, the App supports offline access to locally available school records and entry of marks for assigned subjects and assessments. This means school-related personal information may be stored on the device to support offline work.

Cloud synchronization

When connectivity is available and synchronization is initiated or performed by the App, locally recorded changes may be transmitted to the connected school cloud database, and updated school records may be downloaded to the device. Users should synchronize regularly to reduce the risk of unsynchronized work being lost.

5. Why information is processed

The App processes information to:

  • Validate a participating school's system ID and configure access to its database.
  • Authenticate school-provisioned users and apply school-defined access controls.
  • Show the learners, subjects, classes, and assessment sets relevant to authorized teachers.
  • Enable entry, local saving, and synchronization of academic marks.
  • Maintain consistency between the mobile App, connected cloud records, and the school's management workflow.
  • Support troubleshooting and operation of the service where authorized and necessary.

The App is not described or intended as an advertising platform. This policy does not authorize ERPMobile to sell school records or use them for unrelated marketing.

6. Who can access or receive information

Information may be available to authorized users and administrators of the relevant school, according to school-managed access rights. Information is exchanged with the cloud database and associated services used for that school's synchronization and authentication workflow.

ERPMobile does not independently determine recipients of school academic records. Any developer or service-provider access needed for technical operations or support should be limited to an authorized purpose and appropriate safeguards. The school is responsible for deciding whether to disclose its records to other parties in accordance with applicable law.

This policy does not claim that data is never transmitted to third-party infrastructure: the precise cloud hosting and service-provider arrangements must be assessed as part of the school's deployment.

7. Security and device responsibilities

Cybrun Skool uses school-provisioned access and login controls to restrict use of protected functions. Because the App is designed to work offline, school data may remain on an authorized device between synchronization sessions.

Users should protect their devices with a screen lock, keep account credentials private, avoid sharing devices with unauthorized persons, and synchronize changes when connected. Schools should promptly disable accounts or revoke access when a staff member no longer requires it.

No electronic storage or transmission method is completely risk-free. This policy does not make unverified claims about encryption, independent security audits, or specific technical safeguards. ERPMobile and participating schools should maintain appropriate technical and organizational protections consistent with their respective roles.

8. Retention and deletion of school information

Each school manages retention, correction, and deletion of its database records. Retention periods may depend on the school's policies, legal obligations, and academic recordkeeping requirements. ERPMobile does not independently set or override those retention periods.

The App currently does not include an in-app data-deletion request feature. Users who wish to request deletion or correction of their personal information, or a learner's information where they are authorized to act, should contact the database administrator of the relevant school. That administrator can review the request, verify authority, and take action in the school's systems as appropriate.

Deleting the App from a device is not the same as deleting records held in the school's cloud or desktop database. Local information may also remain in backups or other school-controlled systems according to the school's retention practices. Users should contact their school administrator for the applicable process.

9. Access, correction, and other privacy requests

For requests to access, correct, restrict, or delete school-managed personal data, contact your school's database administrator or designated privacy contact. This includes requests relating to student names, marks, assessment records, and staff account details.

ERPMobile can receive questions about this policy or the App at erpmobilesoftware@gmail.com, but cannot unilaterally change or delete records that are controlled by a school. Where appropriate, ERPMobile may direct a requester to the relevant school administrator.

Rights and procedures may vary under applicable law, including Uganda's data protection framework where relevant.

10. Intended users and learner information

The App is intended for authorized school staff aged 18 or older, not for children to create accounts or use directly. Nevertheless, it may process information about school learners who are under 18. Schools are responsible for establishing the lawful basis and safeguards for handling learner information, limiting access to authorized personnel, and responding to requests from learners, parents, or guardians where applicable.

11. Updates to this policy

ERPMobile may revise this Privacy Policy to reflect changes to the App, its data handling, applicable legal requirements, or connected services. The effective date shown above will be updated when the policy changes. The current version should be made available through the App's public privacy-policy link.
