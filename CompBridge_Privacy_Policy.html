At a glance. CompBridge is a staff-only mobile companion to the DCI Uganda web-based ERP. Staff accounts are created, deactivated and deleted exclusively by authorised DCI administrators through the web-based ERP at https://dcius.org/erp, not through the mobile app. An active internet connection is required. Some information, such as optional saved sign-in credentials, is stored on the device; operational records are sent to and maintained in the organisation's ERP database.

1. Who we are and what this policy covers

CompBridge is a mobile application for authorised personnel of Development Companions International – Uganda (DCI), the owner of the application, its branding and its assets. The application is developed and maintained under the developer name ERPMobile.

This policy describes information handled through the CompBridge application and its connection to the organisation's main DCI Uganda Management Information System (MIS)/ERP. DCI determines staff access and organisational uses of ERP records. ERPMobile is the application developer and technical contact identified below.

The app supports requisitions, learner attendance, academic assessment marks and field surveys. It does not provide public account registration. Users sign in using credentials issued through the DCI ERP administration process. Account creation, deactivation and deletion are performed only by authorised ERP administrators using https://dcius.org/erp.

Staff biodata: Personal information captured during staff account creation on the DCI web-based ERP is stored securely in the organisation’s ERP system and is accessible only to authorised DCI administrators. This is distinct from the limited account and role information that CompBridge uses to authenticate staff and display permitted functions.

Survey responses, videos and other programme records may contain personal information about learners or community members. Staff should only enter or capture information that is necessary and authorised for their work.

3. How information is used

  • To authenticate a staff member against the central ERP and check whether the account remains active.
  • To enforce assigned permissions and limit access to authorised Study Centres, districts, requisitions and other records.
  • To create, retrieve, update or submit requisitions, attendance records, assessment marks and field surveys as permitted by the ERP.
  • To attach mandatory live-camera video evidence to attendance submissions.
  • To maintain operational integrity, respond to support requests and investigate security or misuse concerns where necessary.

CompBridge is designed for organisational programme administration, not public social networking or advertising. Any additional analytics, crash-reporting or third-party SDK data flows introduced in a future release must be assessed and disclosed before deployment.

4. Camera, microphone and biometric access

Camera and microphone

Attendance capture requires a live video recording using the device camera. The camera and microphone are used to capture the visual and audio content of that recording. The resulting video is uploaded to the DCI ERP as attendance evidence. The app should request these permissions when the relevant feature is used. If access is denied, video-based attendance submission may not be available.

Biometrics

Optional fingerprint or facial authentication can be used to unlock saved sign-in credentials on supported devices. Biometric matching is performed by the device operating system; the app is not intended to receive or store raw fingerprint or face templates. A successful biometric check does not grant ERP access on its own: CompBridge must still verify the saved credentials and current account status with the central server. An inactive or invalid account must be denied access.

Other access

CompBridge requires network access to communicate with the DCI ERP. Device and operating-system capabilities may differ. Any future location, file, notification or other sensitive permissions must be disclosed in this policy if added to the application.

5. Where data is stored and how it is protected

On the device: If the staff member enables “Remember credentials,” sign-in information may be retained in platform-protected secure storage. The app may also temporarily hold session information or files needed for its operation. Users should protect their device with a screen lock and sign out when using a shared device.

On DCI's systems: Operational records submitted through the app are transmitted to the organisation's ERP backend and stored in its managed database or associated evidence storage. The ERP controls access based on staff roles and assigned organisational scope.

Connections to the production service should use encrypted HTTPS transport. Staff biodata collected through ERP account creation is accessible only to authorised DCI administrators. Other operational records are subject to ERP role-based access controls. DCI and its technical service providers should apply appropriate server safeguards, backups and maintenance procedures. No electronic system can guarantee absolute security.

CompBridge is not an offline-only app. It needs an active internet connection for server authentication and normal record operations.

6. Who can access information

Information is available to authorised DCI personnel through the ERP according to their assigned privileges, workflow responsibilities and Study Centre or district access. Technical service providers, including ERPMobile where authorised to support the system, may access information only as necessary for their contracted responsibilities and subject to applicable safeguards.

Information may also be disclosed where required by applicable law or a valid legal process. This policy does not claim that all data remains exclusively on the user's device: programme submissions are transmitted to DCI's central systems.

CompBridge is not designed to sell personal information or use programme records for advertising. Any actual third-party hosting, logging, crash-reporting or analytics services should be verified and reflected in this policy and the Google Play Data safety declaration.

7. Retention, correction and deletion

DCI retains staff accounts and programme records according to its operational, safeguarding, audit, contractual and legal requirements. Different records, including attendance evidence and assessments, may have different retention needs. A fixed retention period has not been provided for this application; DCI should confirm and publish its applicable retention schedule before this policy is used as a final production notice.

Staff may request access, correction or deletion of their personal information, subject to DCI's lawful obligations to retain certain organisational records. Account creation, deactivation and deletion are carried out only by authorised DCI ERP administrators through the web portal at https://dcius.org/erp. CompBridge does not provide self-registration or in-app account deletion. Staff who want their account deactivated or deleted must email the DCI system administrator at erp@dcius.org. Deactivating or deleting an account does not necessarily delete related requisitions, attendance evidence, assessment records, surveys or audit records; those records are handled under DCI's applicable retention requirements.

To request account deletion or deactivation, email erp@dcius.org with the subject “CompBridge Account Deletion Request.” For correction of staff biodata, contact the same system administrator. Do not send your password, biometric information or unnecessary learner records by email. The system administrator may verify the requester’s identity and will review and process eligible requests through the ERP. ERPMobile does not independently approve or perform account deletion. DCI should communicate which information can be removed and which must be retained, with the applicable reason.

Users can also remove locally stored CompBridge data through supported in-app sign-out or credential-removal controls where available, or by clearing the app's storage/uninstalling the app. Removing local data does not automatically delete records already submitted to the ERP.

8. Your choices and responsibilities

  • Use only your assigned staff account; do not share your ERP password or saved credentials.
  • Choose whether to enable optional remembered credentials and biometric unlocking.
  • Manage camera, microphone and biometric permissions through device settings, noting that some functions require them.
  • Contact the ERP administrator to correct your profile, review access privileges or raise a concern about programme data.
  • Submit only authorised attendance, assessment, requisition and survey information.

9. Intended users

CompBridge is intended solely for authorised DCI staff members and other approved organisational users who are 18 years of age or older. It is not designed for children or general public registration. Learner records may nevertheless contain information about individuals who are not app users; these records must be handled under DCI's applicable programme privacy and safeguarding procedures.

10. Processing and storage locations

The DCI ERP is accessed through an internet-connected service. The precise locations of the production hosting infrastructure, backups and any service providers have not been confirmed for this policy. DCI should document those locations and any relevant international transfers and safeguards before publication if required by applicable law.

11. Updates to this policy

We may revise this policy when CompBridge's functionality, permissions, service providers or data practices change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated. Material changes should also be communicated through the app or appropriate DCI staff channels when required.
